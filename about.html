Our Story

Engineers of the Autonomous Future.

NETWORX was born out of a simple observation: Businesses are losing the war against manual repetitive tasks. As CSE students immersed in the world of Large Language Models (LLMs) and Agentic Frameworks, we decided to bridge the gap between academic AI and practical business growth.

The Vision

We don't just see AI as a chatbot. We see it as a workforce. Our vision is to empower small to mid-sized enterprises (SMEs) with the same level of automated sophistication used by tech giants.

We are building a future where "Running a Business" means making strategic decisions, while the "Doing" is handled by a network of intelligent agents.

Our Core Pillars:

  • Technical Integrity: Scratch-built code, no generic templates.
  • Agentic Logic: Systems that reason, not just respond.
  • Scalable Systems: Built to grow from 10 to 10,000 tasks.
